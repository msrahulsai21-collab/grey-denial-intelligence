
# GREY Denial Intelligence

### Healthcare Revenue Cycle Management Analytics

GREY is developing practical analytics solutions to help US healthcare billing teams understand denial patterns and improve operational reporting.

## The Problem

Healthcare billing teams need better visibility into:
- Payer-wise denial patterns
- Common denial reasons
- Denied claim amounts
- Recurring operational issues

## Our Proposed Solution

A simple denial analytics dashboard that helps teams:
- Categorise denial reasons
- Analyse payer-level trends
- Summarise denied amounts
- Generate management reports

## Initial Project Scope

- CSV-based data analysis
- Payer-wise reporting
- Denial categorisation
- Dashboard visualisation
- Exportable reports

## Target Customers

US medical billing companies and healthcare RCM service providers.

## Project Status

Early-stage prototype development.

All demonstration data will be synthetic. No patient information or confidential customer data is used.

## About GREY

GREY builds practical technology solutions for healthcare operations and business intelligence.
